# A Dream of a Foolish Man

It was noon, and I was sitting in the park with a storm of thoughts in my mind, clashing against each other like a tsunami inside me. And then I decided: I will not love anyone. Never again, no matter how worthy of love someone may be. Because the one I loved the most is not with me. She didn't stay because of the fears and dangers that come with loving someone. And now the expectations that come with love feel like a burden to me. It breaks my heart and makes me feel worthless. So I made up my mind - I would never love again and I walked back to my apartment.

But when I reached my apartment, a thought crept in: is it really worth punishing yourself by refusing to love? Doesn't a life that never hopes for love become a life lived in vain?

Lost in this thought, I fell asleep and a dream came to me.

The dream took place in a land I had never seen, and yet it felt so familiar — as if I had known this place for thousands of years, as if I came here every single day. And then I saw, beside me, a familiar face: the one I loved the most. She was there. It felt like heaven. It felt like I never wanted to leave this place. It felt like paradise. This was my whole world. We talked for hours. We shared stories for hours. We shared happiness for hours.

But suddenly, something felt wrong because I knew she was not with me anymore. Then how was I with her now? Somehow, she sensed my unease.

She said, "Love is a journey — a journey you should enjoy, not a destination. In this journey there are many ups and downs. You will meet many people you'll come to love, and they will go away. But in the end, what matters is that you keep pouring your love into others. Never stop loving. Never stop caring. You will always find someone who gives that love back to you. If it's not me, it will be someone else — someone with whom you'll live the dreams you dreamed for me, someone who will receive the love you gave me and see the beauty you saw in me. And maybe in some other world, we will be together. Or maybe in some other form, we will be together."

And at last, she kissed me — a kiss that felt like it lasted for centuries, a feeling beyond anything the words of this universe could explain. Then, slowly, everything began to fade away, bit by bit, as if the paradise around me were collapsing into a singularity. At last, it was her turn to dissolve into it — and that was the last time I remembered her face perfectly...

Then I woke up. It didn't feel like a dream, it felt like an entire life I had lived, a life completely different from my own. I could still feel her in my arms. I could still feel her presence around me — her smile, her warmth, and the blush on my cheeks from her kiss. She left me with a light: a light that says love is worth giving, no matter how it ends, and that the journey is worth enjoying far more than the result is worth worrying about.

It was 5:30 in the evening, and outside, a storm was raging. With this new energy, I stepped out to feel the weather. And I must admit after this foolish dream, I love her more than I could have ever imagined. I will carry her light, her words, and the hope that she will come back in some form or another giving back all the love I ever poured into her, and the love she told me I always deserved.
